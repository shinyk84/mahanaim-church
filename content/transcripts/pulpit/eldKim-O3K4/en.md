# Joseph Imprisoned

**Scripture: [[GEN 40:1-15]]**

## Interpretation That Varies Depending on Spiritual State

What is the title of today’s message? It is “Joseph Imprisoned.” Would he have been joyful? Joseph imprisoned—it could seem unfair and become a cause for dispute.

If there were no absolute plan of God, nonbelievers would inevitably see it that way. Yet, even if things appear identical on the outside, someone who possesses the gospel is completely different. What is the most important thing in your eyes? It is changing your spiritual state. Everything changes depending on what kind of spiritual state you possess. Our spiritual state—this is everything.

As soon as you saw this week’s message, didn't the answer come right out? It is about Joseph imprisoned, but what was the daytime message? It was “The Start of the Absolute Journey.” Last week, “The Start of the Absolute Journey” should have come out—since being sold as a slave marks the start of the absolute journey—so why did “The Absolute Goal” come out first? It was preparation for Thanksgiving Sunday. It meant to bear fruit. What is the true goal of our life in our heart of hearts? It is to raise up 7-point disciples: “Go therefore and make disciples of all nations.” This is the purpose.

Therefore, these things must be confirmed. If God has an absolute plan, it is bound to be fulfilled in us. Accordingly, depending on what kind of spiritual state you have, today's reality begins to look different. As for the eyes that see problems, conflicts, and crises, if you naturally think of the 10 Mysteries and embark upon the “Absolute Journey,” the message comes out just like that. Once this is aligned, the message becomes remarkably simple. There is no need to agonize over interpreting it: “Ah, this is the Absolute Journey.”

Next year as well, as you live your life, if the Bartizan is accurately established and your spiritual state is properly set, you will not be deceived by today’s reality. You must understand this with certainty. Problems, incidents, and conflicts constantly come to us every single day. But if you constantly swing between joy and despair over them, your facial expression becomes quite strange. That is what happens when good and bad things repeatedly overlap.

However, the reason we do not have to live that way is that we enjoy God’s blessings day by day. I pray that your life will become one that completely enjoys the blessing of an evangelist. God is with me.

We must always change our spiritual state. Problems, conflicts, incidents, and crises are bound to come in our lives. They come continuously. What should you do if you want to go and live in a world without such problems? You just need to die. There are no words in the grave. If you want to live without problems, you can just go there. But if problems exist in this world, what must we do? We just need to possess the strength capable of overcoming the problems.

That is why we pray, “God, give me strength.” While we do pray for strength, the most important thing is, “Give me eyes to see.” Problems, conflicts, incidents, hardships, and tribulations all exist, but the interpretation differs according to one’s spiritual state. The answer is one. Everything is a matter of interpretation.

When you are imprisoned, do resentment and complaint come out? Why is that? It is because of interpretation. “What did I do wrong to deserve this?” That is also an interpretation. “There is an absolute plan of God.” That is an interpretation as well. The standard for interpretation lies within oneself.

Mental healing is also simple. When you poke an animal, it reacts. It is Stimulus and Response (S-R). But what must exist between stimulus and response? It is thought. When you can think and you have a message, the response changes. In the end, our life is a response to stimuli, but the question is what kind of response comes forth. When a response capable of seeing within the Word comes out, your life will change. You must truly enjoy this answer.

## Christ Who Lives in Me

From the very beginning, we already possessed the answer. It is [[GEN 3:15]]. Why does the Genesis 3 incident come? It is absolutely necessary. It would be nice if it didn't exist, but it is absolutely necessary. Life is an ocean of suffering (*gohae*), a sea of pain. The Buddha also realized that well. But should we be dazed in meditation as a solution? Is taking drugs the best way? No. The answer must come out.

Christ finished all problems. The offspring of the woman crushed the head of the serpent. You must enjoy this answer. It is not about entering into mental ataraxia, nor is it entering into nirvana; you must break Satan, the root cause of the problem. I pray that your life will possess this power.

Do you have the power to break the authority of Satan? Jesus is the Christ. Jesus is the offspring of the woman and the King of kings who shattered Satan's head. He has given us this answer. He gave us authority to tread on serpents and scorpions and to overcome all the power of the enemy.

It is not just, “Ah, I have received authority.” It is that Christ lives in you. This is the walk of faith. It is not forcing yourself to believe by pulling it in; it is that He lives within me. It is enjoying this. It is being together with Christ. Then the other side sees it and knows. This is the walk of faith.

Christ lives inside of me. In the Old Testament, they did not know well about the indwelling of the Holy Spirit, but the moment you accept Jesus Christ, He lives in you forever. How well did the apostle Paul realize this? “Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?” ([[1CO 3:16]]). Isn’t this astonishing? It was something that could never be realized through the law. “The Holy Spirit has built a home in me, so I am God’s temple!” This is a complete blessing. The Holy Spirit is eternal life. We have completely obtained heavenly citizenship.

[[PHI 1:6]] says, “And I am sure of this, that he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.” It is complete. For the apostle Paul, it was completely finished. This is salvation. [[GAL 2:20]]—now, living or dying is no longer an issue. He is a completely different person. The moment these mysteries are confirmed, it is finished. The moment the fact that Christ truly is with me inside is experienced, you come out of everything.

## The Person Who Determined the Future Through the Covenant

Joseph began to confirm this as he listened to the message. It was said that the sun, the moon, and eleven stars would bow down to him. It was finished. His life’s future was already determined.

In [[1SA 16:13]], when oil was poured on David’s head and it was declared that he would become king, it was finished. This is a person who accurately holds the covenant. It is someone who knows the future of their life in advance. God gives us the covenant. He imprints the covenant upon our heart. Once it is imprinted, it is finished, because God fulfills His Word.

It is said that if it is driven into the heart like a peg firmly fixed, the whole weight of Israel's glory will hang on it. The peg of the Word must be firmly driven into you. When hanging something heavy on a wall, if it is not driven in properly, it falls right down. When the Word is driven into your heart like a well-fastened peg, it is fine to hang everything on it. The Word of God does not waver. [[HEB 4:12]] says, “For the word of God is living and active.” When this Word is driven into me, it is finished.

Your life's future must be firmly determined by Christ. Living or dying is already settled. If so, you must confirm that God has called your life as a summit from the very beginning. Only when you know how important of a person you are will you live in an important way. When people go to the reserve forces training camp wearing loose military uniforms with their hats on crooked, they lie down anywhere and urinate anywhere; but if you dress them in a suit, they do not sit down just anywhere, nor do they hang around and fool around anywhere. People act completely differently according to their status. Only when you know that you are an important person will you act differently.

You are one who possesses the blessing to which kings will bow, and only when you confirm the fact that He called you as a summit will you live a life befitting that calling. You go to enjoy the fulfillment of the covenant. It is constantly enjoying this blessing of [[GEN 39:2]], where the Lord was with Joseph.

You must enjoy this through ordinary prayer, lifelong prayer. What is the opposite of ordinary prayer? It is emergency prayer. For a person whose ordinary prayer is in place, heavenly hosts and angels are dispatched in times of emergency prayer. When ordinary prayer is in place, heavenly hosts and angels are dispatched in emergencies, because you are a person of the covenant. But if you lack the foundation, have no covenant, cannot pray, and do not believe in God's absolute sovereignty, you will live in the state of an unbeliever, suffering through every problematic situation. You must completely turn this around. Standing as a Bartizan means that you have the accurate covenant and prayer, and you remain unshaken.

When difficulties come, God is with you. When problems come, prayer takes place. You discover the hidden plan. We are witnesses of the fact that “God is with me.” We are not witnesses of visible miracles or works, but witnesses that “God is with me.”

Do not be mistaken. We do not do it by our own ability, but by the power given from above. ## Imprinting That Turns Scars into Springboards

Look at Joseph in the midst of suffering. In [[GEN 35]], his mother Rachel dies. It is a great scar. The youngest, Benjamin, lost his mother right after being born, so he does not remember his mother well. But for Joseph, who was a few years older and had memories of his mother, her absence was a tremendously difficult problem. The unfilled emptiness and anxiety in the family could easily give rise to an inevitable spiritual problem.

At that time, this had to be confirmed: “For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord will take me in” ([[PSA 27:10]]). This is David’s confession. The moment you meet God, the orphan state ends. The anxious state of fear, worry, and not knowing what will happen comes to an end. God becomes my Father, and the One who created the heavens and the earth protects me. God is my sponsor, my background, and my everything. The moment you enter into the ark, it is finished.

A thought pattern that falls into scars and sinks into depression whenever there is time cannot be cured by any medicine. You must identify what trap makes you fall when you are alone and when you encounter incidents. Every time, you must proclaim Christ before it: “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”

This incident of the mother’s death became the turning point for Jacob to imprint Joseph with the covenant. As he taught the Word and let it take root, Joseph came out of his scars and began to see the vision. Through the 13 years during which the Word was imprinted and confirmed within him, the seventeen-year-old Joseph was given the answer and the dream to conquer the world.

Although his brothers envied him and his father rebuked him, Jacob kept Joseph’s words in mind. It was fine even if he suffered hardships and his brothers tormented him. Throughout that entire process, Joseph continued to gain the answer. Of course, speaking with such conviction stirred up his brothers' jealousy, but you too should be able to declare your life so boldly that it provokes jealousy: “I will certainly succeed. God is with me.”

Whenever an incident occurs, people revert to their old nature, thinking, ‘What good am I? I knew this would happen.’ You must break that. You must break the forces of darkness that drive you back into scars in the name of Jesus Christ! “The curse over me has ended! We will conquer every path we walk!” Declare the answer.

In [[GEN 37]], he faced the crisis of death and was thrown into a pit, but the water was dried up. He was a person who could not die. That time of praying to be rescued from the pit was not mere suffering; it was a time of prayer confirming, ‘I am someone who can never die; God has truly given me the vision.’

For a believer, a problem is an opportunity for prayer and a time to look upon God. It is a time not to look at the bottom of the pit, but to look upward toward God. In the meantime, deep meditation takes place. They tried to kill him, but instead spared him and sold him as a slave. There is a complete plan for our lives. Rather than hiding and sleeping in the bottom of the ship to avoid the storm like Jonah, we must be people who pray like Paul and boldly proclaim the covenant: “For this very night there stood before me an angel of the God to whom I belong and whom I worship.”

Being sold as a slave was not destruction, but the time schedule to go to Egypt. Being falsely accused seemed unfortunate, but it was the time schedule to enter the prison where he would learn politics. It looks like the same suffering, but depending on our spiritual state, everything is interpreted differently. The Lord was with Joseph. When suffering by worldly standards comes, you will never perish.

Because of family problems, it became a time schedule to hold onto the covenant. If there are no problems, people do not pray. How important is the process of the Word being imprinted in me? If the foundation is missing and the spiritual state has collapsed, no matter how many methods you teach, they quickly flip over. Only when people whose spiritual state is truly prepared gather together can it become a lasting Darakbang.

Changing my thoughts is not easy. The Word must be imprinted. You must memorize it and meditate on it. You must look up the original languages, translations, and commentaries to see the deep mysteries of the gospel, and be unified through the covenant of Christ that penetrates the entire Bible. There must be a time of concentration that changes your imprint and nature. The work of changing what is imprinted into your nature—the 62 components of the evangelist’s life—must become yours. Your spiritual state must change.

In [[GEN 39]], Joseph was sold as a slave, but he was not discouraged. It was because it was the time schedule to go to Egypt. Would a father send Joseph, who grew up sheltered, as a missionary to the 237 nation of Egypt? He would not. That is why God dispatched him as a slave missionary. Without needing to spend any money, his food, clothing, and shelter were all provided, and in Potiphar's house, he managed the finances and household while learning the language and administration.

Today, the present, is the best school. If you cannot enjoy this, you will keep wandering around searching for strange places. It is useless to chase after the bluebird. Enjoy the present right now. God is with me, and He gives the best right here.

It seemed that a crisis had arrived, but through the false accusation of General Potiphar's wife, he did not die, but went into the prison where the king's prisoners were confined. Within God’s absolute plan, he entered the time schedule of learning politics. When God finishes having you learn what is needed at this stage, He moves you to the next stage. It was the time schedule to learn politics for world evangelization. Saying, “God, this is so unfair,” is my own thought; the one who knows God’s plan does not agonize.

Finally, he stood before the king. Was it a promotion? No. It was the time schedule to save the world. When famine struck the whole world, people came to buy grain. While wisely managing the economy by bringing the land under state ownership, he explained the Lord God. This is the blessing of one who has the gospel.

## The Absolute Sovereignty of God Who Fulfills the Dream

Sovereignty belongs to God. This is the foundation. Just as in [[1CH 29:10-14]], no matter what crisis or conflict comes, God fulfills His absolute goal. Everything is in God's hands.

Look at [[GEN 40:23]]. The chief cupbearer was restored to office and forgot Joseph. Joseph had pleaded, “For I was indeed stolen out of the land of the Hebrews, and here also I have done nothing that they should put me into the pit. Only remember me, when it is well with you,” but he forgot him.

Is it something to be discouraged about just because the cupbearer forgot Joseph? No. What did Joseph see? He interpreted the cupbearer's dream, and it was fulfilled exactly as spoken. ‘God will also surely fulfill the covenant dream that I had. Even though man may forget me, the Word is surely fulfilled!’ He held onto this.

Even if people forgot him, Joseph saw God’s power that fulfills dreams. That is why later, even when standing before Pharaoh, he could speak boldly: “The doubling of Pharaoh's dream means that the thing is fixed by God, and God will shortly bring it about” ([[GEN 41:32]]). He explained God. Pharaoh confessed, “Can we find a man like this, in whom is the Spirit of God?”

God’s absolute sovereignty—God leads everything perfectly. The most important thing is the fact that the Holy Spirit is God. God lives in me, indwelling, guiding, and working. If so, nothing is a problem.

When does the Holy Spirit work? When you pray. When does the Holy Spirit guide? When you pray. When does the indwelling of the Holy Spirit take place? When you accept Jesus Christ as your Savior. You must enjoy the complete blessing of prayer. Is your ordinary prayer going well? When ordinary prayer is enjoyed—where scheduled prayer takes place, 24 conversational prayer takes place throughout all life relationships, and concentrated prayer takes place in crises—God’s works arise in your life.

God brings about the work of salvation. Rather than evangelism being the purpose, you yourself are evangelism. The very fact that you stand as a Bartizan makes you a witness. If you stand as a Bartizan, you will not collapse. There is no problem with the God of Elijah. The problem is that Elijah, the man of God, was shaken. God is alive, but I am the one who wavers. Your standing as a Bartizan is the start that changes history. Even now, God is carrying out the work of salvation through you and will surely fulfill His covenant.

The covenant being imprinted in me is everything. When was the Word believed in me? ‘I am a child of God, and I am in Christ! Everything in my life has already been given within the covenant of world evangelization!’ The moment this is believed, life changes.

The moment your eyes open, things become different.
