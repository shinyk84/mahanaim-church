# Are You Truly at Peace and Happy?

In life we often find ourselves caught in problems that will not go away.
Family problems, work problems, problems with our children, an uncertain future, and spiritual and physical problems are always before us.
To escape them, some fall into drugs or alcohol, or give themselves over to a reckless life.
Finding no answer, others live by leaning on religion, superstition, shamanism, money, or power.
Why do these problems come? And what, truly, is the solution?

## 1. Man as Originally Created

Of all creation, only man was created in the image of God. Just as fish must live in water, birds in the sky, and trees with their roots in the soil, so man, created in the image of God, must live with God. ([[GEN 1:27-28]])

## 2. Man's Sin

But man was deceived by Satan, sinned through unbelief, and in the end left God. ([[GEN 3:1-6]])
Satan opposes God, brings the curse of suffering, and ultimately destroys man. ([[REV 12:9]], [[ROM 3:23]], [[ROM 6:23]])

## 3. The Fundamental Problems of Man Apart from God

(1) A child of the devil: Apart from God, man is spiritually dead and has come under the rule of the devil. ([[JOH 8:44]])

(2) Idolatry: As a result, people fall into superstition and idolatry, trying fortune-telling, shamanic rituals, and good works, yet find no happiness; unable to choose freely even a day, a direction, or a burial site, they live bound by fate and by the stars of their birth. ([[EXO 20:4-5]])

(3) Suffering of the mind: Gripped by strange curses such as anxiety, discontent, emptiness, mental illness, neurosis, insomnia, and depression, people keep falling into ruin even with a good background, much knowledge, and much money. By nature they are in a state that cannot escape God's wrath. ([[EPH 2:3]], [[MAT 11:28]])

(4) Suffering of the body: People are tormented by incurable diseases, troubles, illness, and nightmares; they suffer sleep paralysis, and their whole body is oppressed with pain though no illness can be named. ([[ACT 8:4-8]])

(5) Death and the judgment of hell: Denying God and clinging only to this present life, man finally dies and faces the eternal judgment of hell. ([[LUK 16:19-31]], [[HEB 9:27]])

(6) Spiritual inheritance: In the end, all these problems are passed down again to our children. ([[1CO 10:20]], [[MAT 27:25]])

## 4. The Only Solution - Jesus Christ

Anyone who truly believes in and receives Jesus Christ can be saved. ([[ROM 10:13]], [[ROM 10:9-10]], [[REV 3:20]], [[JOH 1:12]])

- Jesus Christ ([[ROM 5:8]])
- The true Prophet ([[JOH 14:6]])
- The true Priest ([[ROM 8:2]])
- The true King ([[1JO 3:8]])

## Sincerely pray the following prayer.

God my Father, I am a sinner. Until now I have lived as I pleased. But now I believe that Jesus is the Christ who died on the cross for me and rose again. I open the door of my heart and receive Jesus as my Savior. Please come into my heart and lead me forever. I pray in the name of Jesus. Amen.

## 5. Now That You Are Saved, You Have Received Great Blessings.

(1) You are now a child of God. ([[JOH 1:12]], [[1CO 3:16]])

(2) The Holy Spirit is always with you and leads you. ([[JOH 14:16-27]])

(3) God answers your prayers. ([[JOH 14:13-14]], [[ACT 1:8]])

(4) By the power of Jesus you break the forces of darkness. ([[MAR 3:15]], [[1JO 3:8]], [[MAT 12:28-29]], [[LUK 10:19]])

(5) When you face hardship and pray, God sends His heavenly hosts and angels to protect you. ([[HEB 1:14]], [[REV 8:3-5]], [[PSA 103:20-22]])

(6) As God's people holding heavenly citizenship, you can enjoy its blessings even on this earth. ([[PHI 3:20]])

(7) You have regained the authority to conquer and rule the world with the Gospel. ([[MAT 28:16-20]])
